Found it and fixed it. The scripts were always 5.1-compatible *code* — the problem was the **file encoding**: they were saved as UTF-8 **without a BOM**, and Windows PowerShell 5.1 reads BOM-less files as ANSI. Every em dash, ×, and → in the messages got mangled, which silently broke the parser (pwsh 7 was lenient, so my tests never caught it).

## What I did

- Converted all three `.ps1` files to **UTF-8 with BOM** (the 5.1 standard): the grabber, your TACS splitter, and the PPTX builder — in the workspace and both Desktop copies.
- Re-verified under the **real 5.1 engine** (`powershell.exe`, PS 5.1.26100.9711):
  - ✅ Parse OK — grabber, Split-TacsReport.ps1, Build-Presentation.ps1
  - ✅ Full dry-run executed under 5.1, exit 0, all URLs/requests planned correctly
  - ✅ Hash-synced across workspace / `4x10-DataStudio` / Desktop root
- The HTML studios are browser-based, so PS version doesn't touch them — and every future `.ps1` I ship will be BOM'd UTF-8.
